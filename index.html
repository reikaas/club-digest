Klubbdigest, 18 November 2026

A weekly look at dance floors in Kristiansand and Oslo — house, tech house, techno, disco and the groovy in-between. This first issue covers the weekend of 20–21 November and the nights already announced through winter.

This weekend, 20–21 November

The closest match this weekend to groovy house and tech house is Steve Bug at Jaeger's Sunkissed on Saturday. In Kristiansand, Vaktbua has DJ Friendly with Freddie on Friday, and Foajeen has DJ Stony & Dovenpels on Saturday. Friday in Oslo also brings Fabio & Grooverider for an acid set at Jaeger, and Kamelkollektivet all night at The Villa.
