A weekly look at dance floors in Kristiansand and Oslo: house, tech house, techno, disco and the groovy stuff in between. This issue covers the weekend of 26–29 November and the nights announced so far for December and beyond. Nights marked **New** are appearing here for the first time, and **Updated** means the lineup or ticket details have changed since last week.

## This weekend, 26–29 November

In Kristiansand, Saturday is the night to go out. zvett klubbkollektiv bring organic, melodic and progressive house and techno to Vaktbua, DJ Nonfamous Tomas plays a groovy set at Foajeen, and KlubB SKAKK holds its monthly queer disco at Barrikaden. In Oslo, Friday is the strongest night: Berlin's Ritter Butzke bring Teenage Mutants to Jaeger for tech house, and Randomer plays The Villa with Fochs Koshka live. On Saturday, Rabalder's melodic and tech house trio plays The Villa.

### Kristiansand

**Saturday 28 November — Vaktbua**
[zvett klubbkollektiv: BareTom, L-DaR, Ingemann](https://tikkio.com/events/66998-zvett-klubbkollektiv-vaktbua-klubb)
From 21:00 · 80 kr in the first presale tier, then 120 kr online or at the door (online sales close at 21:00) · 20+
The promised sound is organic house, melodic house and techno, progressive, tech house and psychedelic techno. Of all the Kristiansand nights this weekend, it is the closest to a proper club night.

22:00 · Price not listed

**Saturday 28 November — Barrikaden** · **New**
[KlubB SKAKK (queer disco)](https://www.barrikaden.no/hva-skjer)
21:00–02:30 · Live DJ · Price not listed
The monthly queer disco night run by SKAKK (Skeiv Kultur Kristiansand), for anyone who wants disco and a friendly crowd.
